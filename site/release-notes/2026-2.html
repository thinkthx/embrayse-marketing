What's new

Release 2026.2 – Saved Filters, Improved Dockets, and Knowledge Base

This release introduces saved filter preferences on key screens, a comprehensive new knowledge base for Embrayse, and clearer docket printing to help kitchen staff quickly identify critical dietary information.

Saved Filters on Orders and Prep Pages

Your preferred filter settings, such as location, meal service, group, and sort order, are now saved automatically on the Orders and Prep pages. When you return to these pages, a banner at the top of the page allows you to restore the view instantly, saving you from reconfiguring them each time.

Improved Docket Printing

Dockets now display texture levels and allergy risks more prominently, with highlighted boxes and bold formatting for non-standard textures and known allergies.

Textures on the Prep Report

Both food and drink IDDSI texture levels are now displayed in the resident row on the Prep Report, in addition to the existing texture shown in front of each meal. This means you can always see a resident's textures at a glance, even when there are no drink items on the menu or the resident has no order at all.

Embrayse Knowledge Base

We've published a comprehensive set of help articles for the Embrayse staff app, covering everything staff need to get started and work effectively, including menu planning, order collection, meal prep, and service check-ins. More articles and topics will be added over the coming weeks.

Other Improvements and Bug Fixes

  • Telstra Health Clinical Manager integration now supports mapping additional field types to tags and notes.
  • Clothing Protector is now available as a utensil option.
  • Improved security.
  • Fixed: Adding a new subgroup re-orders the group list.
  • Fixed: Dragging a menu item causes it to appear stretched or squished.
  • Fixed: Inputs on the recipe creation tab lose focus on each letter typed.
  • Fixed: SmithKit API fails with multiple concurrent requests.
  • Fixed: Telstra Health Clinical Manager sync does not clear collection fields when the "Nil" option is selected.
