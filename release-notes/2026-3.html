What's new

Release 2026.3 – Eating & Drinking Status

This release introduces the ability to identify residents who are nil by mouth, helping prevent accidental meal preparation for residents who cannot receive food or drinks orally.

Eating & Drinking Status

Each resident's profile now includes an eating and drinking status, either Oral or Nil by Mouth. When a resident is marked as Nil by Mouth, Embrayse prevents any meal orders from being placed for them, and their food texture on the Prep Report is replaced with a prominent "Nil by Mouth" badge so kitchen staff can clearly identify these residents at a glance.

Other Improvements and Bug Fixes

  • Security updates.
  • Fixed: On Android, the eMeals sign-up screen closes if you briefly switch to another app.
  • Fixed: Printed dockets display resident notes as a single block of text, ignoring line breaks used to separate sections.
  • Fixed: Leecare integration crashes when a resident field contains an unexpected data type.
