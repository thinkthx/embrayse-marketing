Embrayse Meals App Support

Thank you for using the Embrayse Meals app.

For questions about food choices, menu options, dietary needs, or to receive an invitation to place orders, please contact your aged care home directly. They manage resident access and day-to-day meal service.

For technical issues or feedback about the app itself, reach out to us at support@embrayse.com.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Embrayse Meals account at any time from within the app:

  1. Make sure you are logged into the app.
  2. Open the Account menu.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Log in again using the username and password of the account you would like to delete.
  5. Confirm the account deletion.

Your account will be deactivated within 5 minutes, and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.
