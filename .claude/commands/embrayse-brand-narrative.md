# Embrayse — Brand & Audience Context

Use this as the canonical brand reference when writing copy, designing screens, reviewing messaging, or making product decisions.

---

## What Embrayse is

Embrayse is an Australian-made kitchen management software platform purpose-built for residential aged care facilities. It digitises and streamlines food services — managing resident dietary profiles (allergies, textures, medical conditions, preferences), meal ordering, menu planning, docketing, and compliance reporting. The product is sold to aged care providers across Australia (and the UK), currently serving 45+ facilities and 5,000+ beds.

## Core value proposition

**"Aged care food services, run with confidence."**

The software removes complexity rather than digitising it — the key differentiator is that it's genuinely simple to use, to the point where staff with limited computer skills pick it up quickly with minimal training.

## Primary audience (buyers/decision-makers)

Facility managers, general managers, directors of care/operations at residential aged care facilities — particularly multi-site operators and larger providers (500-1,000+ beds). They care about:

- Regulatory compliance (specifically the new Australian Aged Care Quality Standard 6: Food and Nutrition)
- Risk mitigation (allergy/dietary incidents)
- Audit readiness
- Operational efficiency

## Secondary audience (end users)

Kitchen, hospitality, and clinical care staff on the floor. Non-technical. They need software that feels obvious from day one — not another system to fight with.

## Tone & voice

Warm, direct, confident, and human. Not clinical or corporate. Copy uses real language: "your staff will actually want to use it," "kitchen staff relying on memory and hope," "the hardest part is already behind them." It acknowledges the anxiety that comes with compliance and safety responsibilities, then resolves it with reassurance. Australian English throughout.

## Visual/design direction

Clean, modern, warm. Not overly medical or institutional. Imagery centres on care workers and residents in real, humanising moments. UI screenshots are shown in context (iPad, docket-style). Soft cards, subtle gradient orbs, and generous whitespace give it a calm, trustworthy feel.

## Key regulatory context

The revised Australian Aged Care Quality Standards (particularly Standard 6) are creating urgency in the market — providers need to demonstrate food and nutrition compliance. This is a major buying trigger.

- **$5,000 one-time setup fee** per facility
- No lock-in contract
