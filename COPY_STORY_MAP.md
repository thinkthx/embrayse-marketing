## Current Copy Inventory (Condensed)
- Core positioning: all-in-one foodservice platform for senior living and healthcare.
- Core value statement: improve nutrition operations through resident-centric meal management.
- Benefit themes: reduce food costs, lower waste, improve compliance confidence, raise resident/patient satisfaction.
- Workflow promise: menu management, dietary controls, meal ordering, inventory/procurement, reporting, and compliance documentation in one system.
- Proof themes: measurable efficiency, reduced waste, and stronger documentation outcomes.
- eMeals offer: employee meal program powered by payroll deduction, clear PMPM pricing model, and policy controls.
- Integration promise: connect with existing accounting/procurement systems for continuity.
- Trust/support: practical implementation support, app support page, and standard privacy policy coverage.

## Current Story Arc
1. Set the problem: foodservice teams handle high complexity, compliance pressure, and cost constraints.
2. Introduce platform: Embrayse centralizes operational tasks and nutrition-specific workflows.
3. Show outcomes: less waste, better service quality, stronger readiness for audits/surveys.
4. Extend value: employee meal benefit (eMeals) broadens impact beyond resident service.
5. Close with conversion: request demo / learn more.

## Reinterpretation Strategy for the New Site
- Keep the same practical, operations-first narrative.
- Lead with outcomes before feature language.
- Keep claims concrete and low-hype.
- Use one clear CTA per section to reduce decision friction.
- Preserve trust cues: pricing transparency, compliance readiness, and implementation realism.
